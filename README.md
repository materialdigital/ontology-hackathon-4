# 4th PMD Hackathon on Semantic Interoperability

This repository accompanies the **4th PMD Hackathon on Semantic Interoperability**, taking place from **18 to 20 November 2026** ("from Lunch to Lunch") at the **Bundesanstalt fuer Materialforschung und -pruefung (BAM) in Berlin-Steglitz**.

The hackathon is organised within the context of the **Platform MaterialDigital (PMD)** and hosted by **BAM**. Its main focus is the collaborative **further development, extension, and improvement of the PMD Core Ontology (PMDco)**, together with related topics and challenges concerning semantic interoperability in materials science and engineering.

The event provides an open platform for:

- collaborative ontology engineering and modelling;
- discussing and improving concepts and structures of PMDco;
- coordinating contributions from application and domain ontologies;
- developing semantic patterns and reusable modelling approaches;
- exchanging experience between ontology developers, domain experts, and interested newcomers;
- addressing concrete questions, use cases, and challenges contributed by participants;
- documenting decisions, results, deliverables, issues, and pull requests produced during the hackathon.

Participants are explicitly encouraged to bring their own topics, questions, and modelling challenges. These can be discussed and worked on together, drawing on the combined ontology and domain expertise of the group.

## Main Topics

The proposed working topics include:

1. (Re-)organisation of processes and process classes in PMDco
2. Modularisation of PMDco
3. Development of a microstructure module
4. Documentation and application of semantic patterns
5. Resolving the SDC-bearer issue
6. Coordination of concepts that may be uplifted from application or domain ontologies to PMDco

Additional topics and working groups may be introduced by the participants.

## Beginner's Workshop

A dedicated **Beginner's Workshop** is offered especially for doctoral researchers at BAM and other participants who are new to ontologies and semantic technologies. The workshop provides an introduction to ontology fundamentals and practical work with [Protege](https://protege.stanford.edu/).
